AI agents that turn public data into decisions

shifaar is an early-stage AI startup based in Indonesia. We build AI agents that collect, structure, and act on web and marketplace data for online businesses.

What we're building

Our agents automate the research work that teams still do by hand: monitoring competitors and prices across marketplaces, tracking trends on social platforms, and gathering company and market data into clean, usable feeds. The product is in active development and has not been publicly launched yet.

About

shifaar was founded in 2025 in Indonesia. We are a small, self-funded team focused on practical AI agents for commerce and research workflows in Southeast Asia.
